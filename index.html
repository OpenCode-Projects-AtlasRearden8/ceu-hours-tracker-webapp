Important Notes & Disclaimers

  • Education only. This is a personal tracker, not a medical, legal, or licensure tool, and never medical or licensure advice.
  • Not an official record. Your state board of nursing, certifying body, or employer keeps official records and decides what counts. Requirements, hour minimums, and eligible course categories vary by state and credential — verify everything independently before relying on it.
  • No personal data, please. Topic and provider names are normal course details, but keep license numbers, SSNs, patient information, and identifiers out of every field. The printable summary is de-identified by design.
  • Local-only. Everything is stored in this browser's localStorage on this device. No servers, no accounts, no tracking. Clearing browser data erases your log — use Export JSON (backup) regularly.
  • Zero dependencies. Plain HTML/JS/CSS in a single file; it works offline from file://.
